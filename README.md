# AceIT – AI-Powered Interview & Placement Readiness Platform

AceIT is an AI-powered platform designed to help students prepare for campus placements through personalised interview practice, performance evaluation, and placement insights.

It connects student profiles, company requirements, and interview preparation to create a more structured and targeted placement readiness experience.

## Problem Statement

Students often struggle with generic interview preparation resources, limited access to company-specific placement information, lack of realistic interview practice, and insufficient feedback on their performance.

## Solution

AceIT brings portfolio building, placement intelligence, and AI-powered interview preparation together in one platform. Students can explore company requirements, prepare for specific roles, practise interviews, and receive personalised feedback to identify areas for improvement.

## Key Features

- **AI Interview Simulator:** Company-specific technical, HR, and behavioural interview practice.
- **Performance Evaluation:** AI-generated scores, response analysis, and personalised feedback.
- **Student Portfolio Builder:** Organised academic profiles, skills, projects, and certifications.
- **Campus Ledger:** SRM-focused placement insights, company information, eligibility criteria, and placement trends.
- **Personalised Preparation:** Connects student profiles and company requirements with interview preparation.
